## 2. Override writers

### 2.1 The table

Row ids are survey and Table D number (S1-D1 is S1 Table D row D1; S2 rows are numbered 1 to 13 in the survey and written S2-D1 to S2-D13 here). Citations are the surveyors', with S1's evidence column appended to the writer cell.

Column "Type": VALUE = an engineer or admin writes a confirmed or chosen value; CONTROL = starts, stops, retries, deletes or administers; AUTO = automation, or a system effect of other writers, that touches a protected value (listed because the surveys list it in Table D); NONE = the survey found no runtime writer.

Column "WEAKNESS": filled with the survey's own words when its gap cell (S1 "Residual gap", S2 "What does not", S3 "Gap or UNKNOWN", S4 "gap or observation") names a concrete way the protected value can be lost, overwritten, orphaned, reopened, bypassed, changed for other projects, or left without history or guard. Where the gap cell is "n/a", an observation about an absent feature, or a remark with no loss path, the cell says "None stated" and quotes the observation. The surveys do not mark weakness rows themselves: the count of 54 is the scribe's reading of the surveyors' gap cells under that rule.
