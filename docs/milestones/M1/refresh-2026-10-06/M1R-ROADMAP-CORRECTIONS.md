# M1 Refresh: Roadmap and M1 Package Corrections Register

- Date: 6 October 2026
- Repository HEAD: 771001e (the snapshot the surveys describe)
- Roadmap compared: docs/UNIFIED_MASTER_ROADMAP.md, Revision U3, and the accepted M1 package in docs/milestones/M1/ (M1-DATA-OWNERSHIP.csv and .md, M1-DUPLICATION-AND-READ-SIDE-EFFECTS.md)
- Purpose: a list, for the orchestrator, of every place the five surveys found the roadmap or the accepted M1 package wrong, incomplete or imprecise, to carry into roadmap Revision U4. This file does not edit the roadmap or the M1 package.
- Snapshot statement: static reading. Nothing was run and no database was opened. I did not re-verify any citation; file:line references are the surveyors' own.
- Revised 6 October 2026 after the first verification (changes C3 and C7): RC-13, RC-24, RC-27 and RC-58 were corrected; the revising scribe read the code lines those rows now cite.

Sources: S1 interfaces, IFC and floors; S2 drawings, review and redesign; S3 documents, sync, jobs and caches; S4 compliance, knowledge, submittals and workload; S5 GET audit. All are in docs/milestones/M1/refresh-2026-10-06/evidence/surveys/. A reference such as "S4 7.1 row 8" means the eighth row of the table in S4 section 7.1. Paths are under backend/app/ unless they start with backend/ or frontend/. S2, S3 and S4 file abbreviations are expanded the way the companion file M1R-ARTIFACT-CAPABILITY-MAP.md expands them.

Kind. false: the statement is wrong, at least in part. incomplete: true as far as it goes, but leaves out something material. imprecise: the wording or scope misleads. confirmed-with-nuance: the statement stands and the survey adds a limit, a condition or a fact that U4 should carry.

Confidence. "span-checked" means the survey says it checked its cited line spans against the abstract syntax tree (S5 section 0 only). "static reading only" means the survey read the code and ran nothing. The survey files do not say that S1 to S4 were checked mechanically.
